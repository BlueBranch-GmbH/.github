## Open Source

Was sich sinnvoll aus Kundenprojekten herauslösen lässt, veröffentlichen wir hier – aktuell vor allem Contao-Erweiterungen aus unserer eigenen CMS-Arbeit. Alle Pakete sind MIT-lizenziert und über Composer sowie den Contao Manager installierbar.

| Paket | Version | Worum es geht |
|---|---|---|
| [**bilder-alt**](https://github.com/BlueBranch-GmbH/bilder-alt-contao) | [![Packagist](https://img.shields.io/packagist/v/bluebranch/bilder-alt?style=flat-square&label=&color=0036ff)](https://packagist.org/packages/bluebranch/bilder-alt) | Erzeugt beim Bildupload automatisch barrierefreie, SEO-taugliche Alt-Texte und auf Wunsch Seitentitel und Meta-Beschreibungen. |

## In English

**BlueBranch GmbH** is a German app and software agency based in Fürth near Nuremberg, Bavaria. We build native iOS and Android apps, cross-platform apps with Flutter, web applications and the backends behind them – concept, development, store launch and ongoing maintenance. Development happens entirely in-house in Germany and is GDPR-compliant.

Our stack: Swift, Kotlin, Flutter and Dart on mobile; TypeScript, Vue, NestJS, PHP and Symfony on web and backend; Contao and WordPress for content-driven sites. We also build retrieval-based AI chat and search features on top of customer content.

The repositories in this organisation are the parts of our work that generalise beyond a single client – currently MIT-licensed Contao extensions. Issues and pull requests are welcome.
